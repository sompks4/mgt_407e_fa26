data_bundesliga_ghost_raw.csv
All 306 matches of the 2019-20 German Bundesliga season.
Source: football-data.co.uk (https://www.football-data.co.uk/mmz4281/1920/D1.csv),
downloaded 2026-07-02; columns renamed, full-time goals only.

Columns: date, hometeam, awayteam, homegoals, awaygoals,
  ghost = 1 for matches played behind closed doors (no fans) after the COVID-19
  restart on 2020-05-16; ghost = 0 for matches with fans (on or before 2020-03-11,
  when the season was suspended).
224 matches with fans, 82 ghost matches.
