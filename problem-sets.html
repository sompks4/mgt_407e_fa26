What every set has in common

  • 100 points, split across four 25-point problems, with points printed on each lettered part.
  • An “Audit the AI” problem — a deliberately flawed AI transcript you have to diagnose and correct.
  • A checking bonus worth +2 on top of the 100-point scale, for checks you initiate on an AI's output. PS2 requires two distinct checks; see the logistics page of the set for what counts.
  • A pre-upload checklist on the second-to-last page. On PS1, missing answers cost the class more points than any statistical mistake.
  • A formula sheet on the last page, collecting the vocabulary and formula slides from the decks.
