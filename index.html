How to work a problem set

  1. Read the questions. Each set has its own page with the question PDF and the data files it uses.
  2. Do the work one problem at a time. Where a problem is tagged AI Required or AI Allowed, write your own prompts and paste a clean record of the prompt and the output into your answer. Where it is tagged No AI, the reasoning has to be yours.
  3. Assemble your PDF. The answer builder gives you one box per graded part and turns the whole thing into a correctly named PDF. You are welcome to use Word, LaTeX, or scanned handwriting instead — the builder is a convenience, not a requirement.
  4. Upload to Canvas before the deadline, along with any companion files your answers refer to.

Please do not spin your wheels. If you are stuck — on the statistics or on something mechanical like getting a PDF made — reach out to me, to Jacob, or to a classmate. There is no prize for losing thirty minutes to a file format.
